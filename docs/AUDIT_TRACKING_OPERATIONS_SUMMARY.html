Clinical & Simulation Scheduler— Audit Tracking

One-page summary for program staff and faculty

Overview:

Scheduling and tracking app for clinical and simulation hours. Planned scope will support all courses in the ADN program. App can auto schedule students based on clinical/simulation contact hour requirements, and allows for changing clinical requirements in the advanced configuration settings within the app. Faculty can also use the autoscheduling feature to find and schedule makeup days for students when they are absent. At the end of the semester, the app will generate a PDF of report of the semester documenting all student hours. Faculty and admin will then e-sign the PDF and save it to the master repository folder for audit purposes.
