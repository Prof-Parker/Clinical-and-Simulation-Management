        Makeup Day Finder
        
                
        Find makeup slots by joining existing sessions only. Simulation makeup joins weeks where another student already has that Sim (1–17). Clinical makeup prefers joining an existing clinical at the student's oriented facility; Week 18 is last resort for both. Sim-on-clinical-week conflicts prioritize simulation — the clinical is marked missed, then search for clinical makeup (facility join first). Overload slots are makeup-only when a session is at normal capacity.

        Registrar Sections
        
                                
        Registrar section codes (e.g. F6011). Sections are assigned per student in the roster—multiple clinical cohorts may share a section, and students in one cohort may use different sections.
        
                                
        
                            
        
                            
        
                                
        Clinical Faculty
        
                                
        Type a name to search adjunct faculty from the user registry (when connected).
        
                                
                                
        
                            
        
                            
        
                                
        Simulation Faculty
        
                                
        Instructors who teach simulation sessions (distinct from student sim roles). Used on the Theory Master Calendar faculty display.
        
                                
        
                            
        
                            
        
                                
        Course Staff
        
                                
        Lead course faculty attests makeup records at end-of-semester closeout (see Audit tab). Not the same as per-group clinical faculty above.
        
                                
        
                                    
                                    
        
                                        
                                        
                                    
        
                                    
                                    
                                
        
                                
        
                            
        
                        
        
                        
        
                            
        Facilities & Clinical Groups
        
                            
        One entry per clinical site. A clinical group may use one site all semester or multiple sites (clinical weeks rotate across listed sites in order).
        
                            
        
                            
        
                            
        
                                
        Clinical groups
        
                            
        
                            
        
                            
        
                            
        
                                
        Simulation groups
        
                            
        
                            
        Primary weekday and even/odd week pattern per simulation group. Count may differ from clinical groups; when counts match, regenerate aligns C1→SG1, etc.
        
                            
        
                            
        
                                
        Simulation times
        
                                
        Default session window for calendars and audit hours. Optional overrides per sim number.
        
                                
        
                                    
                                    
                                
        
                                
        
                            
        
                            
        Simulation days
        
                            
        Days of the week when simulation sessions may be scheduled.
        
                            
        
                        
        
                    
        
                
        
                
        
                    
        Orientation Days
        
                    
        One orientation date per clinical group and site. Master schedule shows “Orient SRMC” style labels.
