      Makeup Day Finder
      
                
      Find makeup slots by joining existing sessions only. For a missed clinical, select which clinical week was missed, then apply a join at the student's oriented facility (Week 18 last resort). For a missed simulation, choose Simulation 1–5 (labels include the student's originally scheduled week), then apply a join where another student already has that Sim (weeks 1–17; Week 18 last resort). Sim-on-clinical-week conflicts prioritize simulation — the clinical is marked missed, then search for clinical makeup (facility join first). Overload slots are makeup-only when a session is at normal capacity.

      Clinical
      
                            
      
                                
      Facilities
      
                                
      One entry per clinical site. A clinical group may use one site all semester or multiple sites (clinical weeks rotate across listed sites in order).
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Starting weeks
      
                                
      Per clinical group start week. Simulation still uses Simulation start week in Advanced Configuration.
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Clinical groups
      
                                
      
                            
      
                        
      
                        
      
                            
      Simulation
      
                            
      
                                
      Simulation groups
      
                                
      Primary weekday and even/odd week pattern per simulation group. Count may differ from clinical groups; when counts match, regenerate aligns C1→SG1, etc.
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Simulation times
      
                                
      Default session window for calendars and audit hours. Optional overrides per sim number. Lunch break is deducted only from Coordinator contact-hour totals (student calendars still show full start–end).
      
                                
      
                                    
                                    
                                    
                                
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Simulation content tags
      
                                
      Specialty tags per sim number (MS → REGN 35P; OB/PEDS → REGN 36P). Used for audit hour subtotals and student calendar labels.
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Simulation days
      
                                
      Days of the week when simulation sessions may be scheduled.
      
                                
      
                            
      
                        
      
                        
      
                            
      Faculty
      
                            
                            
      
                                
      Clinical Faculty
      
                                
      Type a name to search adjunct faculty from the user registry (when connected).
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Simulation Faculty
      
                                
      Instructors who teach simulation sessions (distinct from student sim roles). Used on the Theory Master Calendar faculty display.
      
                                
      
                            
      
                            
      
                                
      Course Staff
      
                                
      Full time faculty attests makeup records at end-of-semester closeout (see Audit tab). Not the same as per-group clinical faculty above.
      
                                
      
                                    
                                    
      
                                        
                                        
                                    
      
                                    
                                    
                                
      
                                
      
                            
      
                        
      
                        
      
                            
      Registrar
      
                            
      
                                
      Registrar Sections
      
                                
      Registrar section codes (e.g. F6011). Sections are assigned per student in the roster—multiple clinical cohorts may share a section, and students in one cohort may use different sections.
      
                                
      
                            
      
                        
      
                    
      
                
      
                
      
                    
      Orientation Days
      
                    
      One orientation date per clinical group and site. Master schedule shows “Orient SRMC” style labels.
      
                    
      
                
      
            
      
            
      
                
      
                    
      
                        
      Student Roster
      
                        
      Drag students between cohorts. Set each student’s registrar section individually, or use the cohort Section / Sim controls to assign all at once (you can still split students afterward).
